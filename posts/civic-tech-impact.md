Report2Clean transforms waste management in Biratnagar by connecting citizens directly with municipal clean-up teams through real-time technology:

Instant GPS Hotspot Reporting: Residents capture photos and geotag exact garbage locations, eliminating guesswork for city crews.

Smart Dispatcher Routing: A centralized dashboard assigns reports directly to local ward sanitation teams for quick collection.

Transparent Track & Resolve: Citizens track the cleanup status live, building trust and civic accountability across Biratnagar.
